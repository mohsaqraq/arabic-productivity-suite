# Arabic Productivity Suite

Four independent, open-source Android apps for budgets, journaling, schedules, and notes. All four work offline without an account. The interfaces are designed for Arabic and right-to-left layouts. App data stays on the device, and users can export or back it up from Settings.

[العربية](README.md) · [APK downloads](downloads/)

| App | What it does | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Almahfaza](apps/Almahfaza) | Expense tracking, monthly budgets, and optional spending alerts | 7.0+ |
| [Alyawmiyat](apps/Alyawmiyat) | Text or handwritten journal entries, photos, and reflection prompts | 7.0+ |
| [Altaqwim](apps/Altaqwim) | Calendar views, appointments, and tasks with priorities | 8.0+ |
| [Almolahazat](apps/Almolahazat) | Notes, checklists, tags, photos, and Arabic-aware search | 8.0+ |

Open an app folder as a standalone project in Android Studio. See [build and contribution instructions](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [Apache 2.0 license](LICENSE). Bundled fonts and third-party components retain their own licenses.
